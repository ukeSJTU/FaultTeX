\begin{abstract}
Scientific claim verification requires models to combine evidence from sources that
vary in relevance and reliability. We introduce Calibrated Evidence Aggregation (CEA),
a lightweight verifier that jointly estimates sentence relevance and source confidence
before predicting whether a claim is supported. On EvidenceBench, CEA reaches an
accuracy of $84.6\%$ while retaining competitive calibration error. Controlled
experiments show that the proposed method improves verification accuracy when evidence
sources disagree.
\end{abstract}
